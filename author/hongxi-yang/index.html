Hongxi Yang

PhD Candidate | Department of Data Science and AI at Monash University | AIM for Health Lab | Melbourne, Australia

Monash University

Biography

PhD candidate in the AIM for Health Lab, Department of Data Science and AI at Monash University. Her research focuses on computer vision, multi-modal learning, generative modelling, and explainable AI for medical image analysis. Her work spans latent diffusion models for controllable 3D image synthesis, vision-language model reasoning for interpretable diagnosis, and clinical decision support systems. She has published in venues including AJNR, IEEE ISBI, and ISMRM, and serves as a reviewer for MICCAI.
